Features

Everything you need to run a hotspot

From live connection monitoring to voucher printing, Bandofy gives you the tools to manage your Wi-Fi business effortlessly.

Live Monitoring
See who's connected right now — device count, bandwidth usage, and live revenue flowing in.
Voucher Sales
Generate, print, and sell time-based vouchers. Set your own packages and prices.
Revenue Analytics
Daily and monthly revenue charts, transaction history, and package performance insights.
Mobile Payments
Accept M-Pesa payments directly on your hotspot portal. Customers pay and connect instantly.
Custom Packages
Define hourly, daily, and weekly plans. Set bandwidth limits for each tier.
Ad Campaigns
Show ads on your portal page and track impressions. Monetise your hotspot beyond data sales.
Branded Portal
Upload your logo, choose your accent color and tagline. Your hotspot looks like your brand, not a generic page.
Device Management
Register every access point by MAC address. Choose Omada or Ruijie integration per AP, toggle active status anytime.
Staff Vouchers
Create reusable codes for staff. Limit by device count, set daily session minutes, no payment needed.
How It Works

From signup to live hotspot in minutes

Four simple steps to start selling Wi-Fi and tracking your revenue.

1
Sign Up
Create a free account with your name, email and hotspot name. Your 7-day trial starts immediately — no card needed.
2
Connect Your Hardware
Add your Omada or Ruijie access points by MAC address. Enter your controller's Site ID or AC name.
3
Build Your Packages
Set hourly, daily or weekly plans with your prices. Add bandwidth limits and device caps per plan.
4
Go Live
Guests see your branded portal, pay via M-Pesa or Airtel Money, and connect instantly. Watch revenue come in.
Hardware Support

Works with your existing equipment

Bandofy integrates with two major hotspot controller platforms — no new hardware required.

Supported
TP-Link Omada
Connect your Omada Controller. Bandofy provisions the guest SSID and manages authorisations through the Omada API — no manual portal setup.
Supported
Ruijie AC / Reyee
Uses Bandofy's built-in RADIUS server. No separate controller required — the AC authenticates customers directly against Bandofy.

More hardware integrations coming soon. Contact us if your brand is not listed.
